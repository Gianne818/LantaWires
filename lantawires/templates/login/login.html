LantaWires

Overhead Wire Hazard Detection System

Empowering Cebu City residents to identify, analyze, and report dangerous sagging cables and tangled spaghetti wire clusters.

In dense urban corridors and interior barangay roads across Cebu City—from Katipunan in Tisa to V. Rama in Guadalupe—low-hanging, overgrown wire bundles cause traffic entanglements, road blockages, and severe fire hazards.

LantaWires provides an independent civic tool where community members can photograph hazardous wires, obtain instant computer-vision clearance estimations, and track resolution progress across all Cebu City neighborhoods.

AI Hazard Triage

Calculates cable sag height and bundle density from uploaded photos.

GPS Barangay Map

Maps verified wire hazards across all 80 Cebu City barangays.

Ticket Tracking

Follow reports from submission to inspection and bundle clearing.
